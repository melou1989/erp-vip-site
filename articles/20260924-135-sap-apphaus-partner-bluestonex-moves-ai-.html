SAP AppHaus Partner Bluestonex Moves AI to Hands-On Demos

PUBLISHED September 24, 2026 · NEWS / Partnership

Bluestonex, a partner in the SAP AppHaus Network, is framing its work as a bridge between AI inspiration and hands-on implementation. The company is using two showcases to show what that looks like in practice, according to the announcement, with retail and supply chain as the industries in view.

The retail and supply chain focus is telling. Both are areas where ERP systems already hold operational data that AI projects need, and both can expose whether a demo actually changes a workflow or just looks good in a workshop. The summary does not say which applications are included, only that the showcases cover different scenarios.

The available details stop at the showcase level. No customers, deployment numbers, or timelines were included in the headline or summary. What is clear is that Bluestonex is using the AppHaus Network to move AI discussions toward something teams can actually test.
