Procurement's Five-Year Shift: Ambition to Measurable Value

PUBLISHED September 17, 2026 · NEWS / Industry Research

The Economist Enterprise research covers five years of procurement change, tracing a shift from digital ambition to operational accountability. According to the summary, the focus has moved toward value that can be measured rather than transformation promises alone.

Pandemic disruption and AI at scale both sit inside that arc. Procurement teams first had to digitize under pressure, then faced a harder question: what did the technology actually change in day-to-day operations?

In practice, that puts ERP and procurement software vendors on the defensive. Buyers want evidence of operational impact, not another broad transformation story. The research points to accountability as the new baseline.
