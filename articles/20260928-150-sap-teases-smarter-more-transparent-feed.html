SAP Teases Smarter, More Transparent Feedback Experience

PUBLISHED September 28, 2026 · NEWS / Product Update

SAP is working on a new customer feedback experience, which it describes as smarter and more transparent. The company has not shared specific timing or feature details beyond that description.

The underlying message is not entirely new: SAP says customer feedback has always been part of how it builds software. What changes here is the promise of a better feedback loop — one that customers can see and understand more easily.

For ERP customers, that matters because feedback tools often sit between a support ticket and a product roadmap. If SAP follows through, the result could affect how requests are submitted, tracked, and answered. Until more details arrive, though, it is mostly a signal of intent.
