SAP Launches Advanced Success Plan for B2B Omnichannel Commerce

PUBLISHED July 29, 2026 · NEWS / Product Update

SAP has introduced what it calls an Advanced Success Plan for its Customer Experience suite, aimed squarely at B2B companies wrestling with omnichannel commerce. The plan, according to the announcement, is designed to help these businesses manage customer interactions across multiple touchpoints.

The company said modern B2B buyers now expect the same fluid experience whether they are on a website, using a mobile app, browsing a marketplace, or working through a partner portal. That expectation has put pressure on ERP and CRM systems to keep pace. The new plan seems to bundle tools and services to address that gap.

Honestly, this is a logical move for SAP, given how many of its customers operate in B2B distribution and manufacturing. The Advanced Success Plan name suggests a premium tier of support or consulting — the company didn't share pricing specifics, but it's likely aimed at larger enterprises.
