Salling Group Puts SAP Cloud ERP at the Center of Its AI Push

PUBLISHED August 04, 2026 · NEWS / Digital Transformation

Salling Group, the European retailer behind a range of grocery and discount formats, is treating SAP Cloud ERP as the launchpad for its AI work. The company said the cloud backbone will let it apply AI at scale in everyday retail rather than in isolated experiments.

What that looks like in practice likely involves areas such as demand forecasting, inventory, pricing, and store-level tasks where AI can act on live ERP data. The company has not detailed every use case in this particular update, but the direction is clear: AI is being tied to core systems, not bolted on.

For other retailers, the move is another example of AI value depending less on standalone models and more on the quality and flow of back-end data. Salling's emphasis on SAP Cloud ERP suggests that the promised AI payoff in retail will be won at the systems layer first.
