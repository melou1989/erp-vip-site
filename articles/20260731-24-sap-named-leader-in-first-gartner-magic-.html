SAP Named Leader in First Gartner Magic Quadrant for Digital Twins

PUBLISHED July 31, 2026 · NEWS / Analyst Recognition

SAP has been named a Leader in the first Gartner Magic Quadrant for Digital Twin of an Organization Platforms. The company said its offering gives businesses a live digital replica of the organization, with continuous observability and AI-driven actionability built in.

What that means in practice: instead of a one-time model, the platform keeps refreshing itself as business conditions change. When something drifts, the AI layer can surface what's happening and point toward next steps — or in some cases trigger a response directly.

Gartner's decision to create a dedicated Magic Quadrant for this category is a sign that digital twin technology has moved past shop-floor pilots into mainline enterprise planning. Whether that matters for your ERP roadmap depends on how much of your operation you need to observe in real time.
