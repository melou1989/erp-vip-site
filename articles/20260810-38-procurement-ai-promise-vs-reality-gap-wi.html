Procurement AI: Promise vs. Reality Gap Widens

PUBLISHED August 10, 2026 · NEWS / Industry Analysis

AI is getting a lot of attention in procurement circles these days — some of it warranted, some of it not. The core issue isn't the technology itself, but the distance between what AI is supposed to do and what it actually does inside a procurement operation.

The dual role is becoming hard to ignore. On one side, AI can automate routine sourcing tasks and spot patterns in spend data. On the other, the same tools introduce new complexity — data quality problems, unclear processes, and teams that don't trust the output. That reality gap is only growing wider.

What needs to change? Less emphasis on flashy pitches, more focus on practical implementation. According to the analysis, procurement leaders need to start treating AI as an operational challenge, not just a technology decision.
