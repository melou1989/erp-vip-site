SAP Commerce Cloud and Vercel team up for faster storefront delivery

PUBLISHED September 04, 2026 · NEWS / Partnership

SAP is tying Commerce Cloud to Vercel's front-end platform. The pitch is simple: teams get a faster route from commerce backend work to the pages customers actually touch.

Commerce Cloud brings the familiar catalog, pricing, cart, and customer data machinery. Vercel brings modern frontend delivery and a developer-friendly workflow. Together, they give e-commerce teams more room to shape the storefront experience without ripping out the commerce engine underneath.

Whether that translates into better customer outcomes will depend on how well the two systems work together outside of a keynote demo. But the direction is clear: SAP is making room for external frontend tooling, and that is a meaningful shift for commerce teams that felt locked into the default storefront stack.
