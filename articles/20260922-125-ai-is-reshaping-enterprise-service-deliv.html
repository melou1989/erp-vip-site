AI Is Reshaping Enterprise Service Delivery

PUBLISHED September 22, 2026 · NEWS / Industry Trends

The headline points to a broad shift: AI is altering how enterprise software services get delivered, not just how the software itself works. For ERP, that covers implementation, integration, training, and ongoing support—the parts of a project that often decide whether the system earns its keep.

Vendors may use AI to automate diagnostics, route support issues, and guide users through tasks. Buyers may expect faster answers and more self-service. The raw item doesn't name specific products, vendors, or timelines, so those are directional expectations, not confirmed details.

Honestly, the hard part isn't the AI pitch. It's governance, data quality, and whether service teams actually change their workflows. If they just bolt AI onto the same process, little improves. ERP customers should ask how service delivery changes day to day—and who owns the data and decisions.
