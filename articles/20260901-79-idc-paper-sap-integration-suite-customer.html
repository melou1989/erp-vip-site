IDC Paper: SAP Integration Suite Customers See 368% ROI

PUBLISHED September 01, 2026 · NEWS / Industry Report

A new white paper from IDC makes a direct financial case for SAP Integration Suite. According to the research, customers typically achieve a 368% return on investment and reach payback in eight months.

The study's wider point is that integration isn't just an IT cost. In a modern enterprise, it touches application connectivity, data movement, partner workflows — and the paper argues the measurable benefits show up across all of those areas.

The figures will inevitably get attention, and they're tied to a specific vendor's tooling. But the broader takeaway is that integration platforms are increasingly being assessed on business value, not just technical capability.
