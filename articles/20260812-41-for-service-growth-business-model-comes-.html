For Service Growth, Business Model Comes First

PUBLISHED August 12, 2026 · NEWS / Business Strategy

The endless parade of service-tools tends to obscure a simpler truth: if the underlying business model is flawed, no piece of software will fix it.

According to the announcement, technology can help scale a service business, but it does not by itself decide how value is created, delivered, or monetized. Those choices live upstream, in the model itself.

In practice, that means making the business model the starting point — then deciding what software, if any, deserves a budget. It's a healthy correction for anyone tempted to buy their way out of a strategy problem.
