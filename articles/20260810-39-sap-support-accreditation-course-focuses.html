SAP Support Accreditation Course Focuses on AI-Powered Help

PUBLISHED August 10, 2026 · NEWS / Product Update

SAP is adding an enablement course to its support accreditation track, with the stated aim of making customer support faster and more AI-driven. According to the announcement, the training is meant to help service teams resolve issues on their own instead of escalating everything.

The interesting part is the emphasis on working independently. This doesn’t look like a generic certification module; it reads as an attempt to get support staff comfortable using AI as part of their normal workflow.

For now, the move signals that SAP wants accreditation to reflect practical AI skills, not just product knowledge.
