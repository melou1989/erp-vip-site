SAP Cleared: German Cartel Office Won't Open Antitrust Case

PUBLISHED August 01, 2026 · NEWS / Regulatory

SAP says it welcomes the German Federal Cartel Office's decision not to initiate antitrust proceedings against the company. The announcement was made in a brief statement from its Walldorf headquarters.

The Bundeskartellamt, as the regulator is known, concluded its examination without opening a formal case. The company's statement does not go into detail about what triggered the review or what the authority ultimately examined.

For SAP, this removes a regulatory question that had been hanging over its operations in Germany. The ERP vendor had no further comment beyond welcoming the outcome.
