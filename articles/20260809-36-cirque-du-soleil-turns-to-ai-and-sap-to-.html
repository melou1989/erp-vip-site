Cirque du Soleil turns to AI and SAP to reinvent itself

PUBLISHED August 09, 2026 · NEWS / Digital Transformation

Cirque du Soleil has built a reputation on reinventing circus arts, and it now says the same instinct is shaping its back-office technology. The company described its work with SAP as a way to keep operations running while simultaneously rethinking them, with AI playing a central role in that effort.

According to the announcement, the entertainment company isn't treating AI as a separate initiative bolted onto its ERP systems. It sees the technology as part of the same continuous renewal that has driven its shows for decades. What that means in practice: SAP is not just the system of record, but also a platform for experimenting with how Cirque delivers its business.

No details on specific AI tools or timelines were included in the announcement. The point, for now, is less about the technology and more about the attitude: Cirque says reinvention is in its DNA, and SAP is one of the places where that plays out.
