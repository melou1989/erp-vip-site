2027 SAP Innovation Awards Open for Submissions

PUBLISHED September 28, 2026 · NEWS / Awards

SAP has opened submissions for the 2027 SAP Innovation Awards. The program is aimed at organizations that take on difficult business problems with SAP technology and can point to measurable results, according to the announcement.

The awards focus on outcomes, not just on whether an SAP system went live. Entrants have to show what changed in the business as a result of the work, according to the awards description.

Teams interested in entering should check the official awards page for deadlines, eligibility rules, and submission requirements.
