SAP Links AI Agent Scale to Business Transformation Management

PUBLISHED September 22, 2026 · NEWS / Product Strategy

SAP is pitching its Business Transformation Management offerings as the connective layer for the autonomous enterprise. According to the company, these solutions are meant to help AI agents work at scale—not as isolated experiments, but as part of day-to-day operations.

That framing puts the emphasis on process, data, and oversight rather than model hype. AI agents can only take action inside a business if the surrounding ERP workflows give them something to work with and guardrails to work inside.

In practice, the value will hinge on how tightly SAP connects these management tools to live ERP workflows. The signal is that agents are being treated as operational infrastructure, not side projects.
