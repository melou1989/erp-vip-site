SAP Business AI Hits Q2 2026: What's New

PUBLISHED July 27, 2026 · NEWS / Product Update

SAP has released its Q2 2026 update for Business AI, the company’s suite of AI tools embedded across its ERP ecosystem. According to the announcement, the release focuses on making routine decisions faster and automating repetitive workflows that typically eat up staff time.

The update includes enhancements to how AI models interact with core business processes — think procurement, finance, and supply chain. Rather than just generating reports, the new tools are designed to suggest actions and flag exceptions before they become problems. The company said these capabilities are meant to help teams shift attention from data entry to higher-value work.

Honestly, this is less about flashy new features and more about tightening the connection between AI suggestions and actual execution. Whether that holds up in practice will depend on how well the models handle messy, real-world data.
