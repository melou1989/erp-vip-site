Agentic AI's Real Test: Trusted Enterprise Data

PUBLISHED September 23, 2026 · NEWS / AI Governance

The headline frames a familiar problem for ERP teams: agentic AI can plan and take action, but it is only as good as the data it is allowed to see and change. Trusted enterprise data is not just clean records. It also means permissions, lineage, timeliness, and agreement across systems about the same customer, item, or supplier.

The underlying concern is trust in the environment where an agent works. That is a governance question as much as a model question. If an agent reads a record without knowing whether it is current, approved, or safe to act on, autonomy becomes a liability.

In practice, ERP leaders will ask what the agent did, what data it used, and who remains accountable when something breaks. Vendors can market autonomous workflows, but finance, supply chain, and IT teams will judge adoption on whether the surrounding data environment can be trusted. Until that is clear, agentic AI stays closer to a pilot than a production system.
