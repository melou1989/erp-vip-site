SAP CX's TrustRadius 2026 Win Signals Agentic AI Momentum

PUBLISHED August 27, 2026 · NEWS / Award / Recognition

SAP's Customer Experience unit has picked up a TrustRadius Top Rated 2026 award, the company said. The recognition comes as SAP makes a bigger push into agentic AI — the catch-all term for AI systems that can take action on their own rather than just generate responses.

TrustRadius ratings are built from customer reviews, and the announcement said those reviews made a consistent point: SAP CX holds up in real deployments. Reviewers across sales, service, and commerce emphasized depth of integration, reliability at enterprise scale, and AI workflows with tangible business impact.

The award is a useful signal for SAP's CX roadmap. TrustRadius's Top Rated tag comes from people who use the software day-to-day, which makes it harder to dismiss. The open question is whether SAP can hold that trust while pushing agentic AI into its CX portfolio without adding complexity.
