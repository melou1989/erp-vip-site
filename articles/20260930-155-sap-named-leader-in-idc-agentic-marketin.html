SAP named Leader in IDC agentic marketing assessment

PUBLISHED September 30, 2026 · NEWS / Analyst Recognition

SAP has been named a Leader in the IDC MarketScape: Worldwide Agentic Marketing for B2C Enterprises 2026 Vendor Assessment. The company tied the recognition to its broader effort to help enterprises deliver intelligent, personalized customer experiences, according to the announcement.

The IDC MarketScape evaluates vendors in a specific market segment. Here, that segment is agentic marketing aimed at B2C enterprises — software that can take on marketing tasks with some degree of autonomy. The headline does not include scoring details, the full vendor list, or any new product announcements.

For SAP, the placement adds another analyst recognition in a category that overlaps with its customer experience and enterprise applications work. In practice, B2C companies considering agentic marketing tools will want to see how those capabilities connect to the systems they already run. The announcement itself stays at the level of market positioning.
