SAP and NHL Team Up for Next-Gen Hockey

PUBLISHED July 28, 2026 · NEWS / Partnership

SAP is taking its corporate partnership with the National Hockey League beyond the rink. According to the announcement, the two organizations are broadening their collaboration to focus on developing the next generation of players, fans, and community leaders.

The initiative targets classrooms, community programs, and innovation hubs — not just ice rinks. In practice, that means technology and resources from SAP will support youth hockey development and educational efforts, though the companies did not share exact funding or program details.
