Lockheed Martin Picks SAP SuccessFactors for HR Overhaul

PUBLISHED September 08, 2026 · NEWS / Implementation

Lockheed Martin says it is moving its human resources systems onto SAP SuccessFactors, part of a workforce transformation the defense contractor has been working on. The news came via a brief announcement, with no financial details or timeline attached—just confirmation that SAP is becoming the company's HR platform.

SuccessFactors is SAP's cloud-based HR suite, covering recruiting, onboarding, learning, and performance management from one system rather than scattered across older tools. For a company of Lockheed's scale and regulatory exposure, that sort of consolidation is a significant operational shift, not just a routine software update.

The real test, as with any large ERP migration, will be in the data cleanup and transition. But the direction here is clear enough: Lockheed is standardizing on SAP's cloud HR stack as part of a broader push to modernize how its people operations actually run.
