SAP Certification Hits 30 Years of Skills Validation

PUBLISHED September 11, 2026 · NEWS / Certification Milestone

SAP Certification has reached its 30-year milestone. The program is designed to let people in the SAP ecosystem prove their expertise and show what they can do, according to the announcement.

Over that time, the certification has served as a common reference point for consultants, administrators, developers, and others whose jobs revolve around SAP software. The announcement frames the anniversary as a moment to recognize how the credential has adapted to changes in work.

Plenty of certifications come and go. SAP's has lasted three decades, which says something about how entrenched the platform is in enterprise operations. The core promise remains narrow and practical: a way to verify skills. Whether that promise stays relevant as SAP's technology and customer expectations shift is the harder part.
