SAP to Acquire TechWolf, Boost SuccessFactors Work Insights

PUBLISHED October 06, 2026 · NEWS / Acquisition

SAP said it plans to acquire TechWolf. According to the announcement, TechWolf is expected to become an intelligent core of the SAP SuccessFactors portfolio.

The stated goal is to give enterprises an evidence-based view of work as AI changes how roles and skills are understood. In practice, that pushes work and skills data further into the center of SAP's HR software strategy.

The announcement carried Walldorf and Ghent datelines. For SuccessFactors customers, the deal points to a more data-driven layer for understanding what work actually happens across the organization.
