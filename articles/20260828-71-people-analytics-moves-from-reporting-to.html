People Analytics Moves from Reporting to Strategy

PUBLISHED August 28, 2026 · NEWS / Trend Analysis

People analytics has spent years living in the rearview mirror. It produced turnover reports, headcount summaries, and the occasional retention graph for the board. That role is now being redefined.

The shift is toward using workforce data to shape decisions before they're made — not just explaining what already happened. That means HR and ERP teams are being asked to connect people data to operational planning in ways that many haven't had to before.

What's driving this is a growing expectation that workforce choices deserve the same analytical rigour as finance or supply chain. Tools that treat people analytics as an afterthought will likely feel the pressure sooner rather than later.
