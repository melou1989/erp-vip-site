SAP Named a Leader in First Gartner Magic Quadrant for SCM Suites

PUBLISHED August 15, 2026 · NEWS / Analyst Recognition

SAP is a Leader in the first Gartner Magic Quadrant for supply chain management suites, the company announced. The new report marks the first time Gartner has ranked vendors specifically across a full SCM suite.

SAP's positioning is tied to its long-running work in the space — the company said it has spent more than 50 years helping organizations link planning, execution, and business processes to cope with supply chain volatility. The result lands as supply chain software gets renewed attention from enterprises.
