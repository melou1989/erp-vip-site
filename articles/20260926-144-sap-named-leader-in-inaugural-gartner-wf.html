SAP named Leader in inaugural Gartner WFM Magic Quadrant

PUBLISHED September 26, 2026 · NEWS / Analyst Recognition

SAP has been recognized as a Leader in the first Gartner Magic Quadrant for Workforce Management Technology, according to the announcement. The analyst firm's inaugural MQ in this category places SAP among the vendors it considers leaders.

The announcement frames the moment around a broader need: organizations want more than scheduling tools and time systems. They want a connected understanding of their workforce. That is a wide ask, and it explains why Gartner created a separate MQ for this space.

For SAP, the recognition is an early marker rather than a long-established benchmark, since this is Gartner's first MQ for the category. Buyers comparing workforce management vendors now have another reference point to weigh.
