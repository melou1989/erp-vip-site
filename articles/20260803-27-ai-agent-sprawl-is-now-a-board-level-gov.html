AI Agent Sprawl Is Now a Board-Level Governance Problem

PUBLISHED August 03, 2026 · NEWS / AI Governance

The rapid spread of AI agents across business workflows has turned governance from an IT checklist item into a board-level concern. The pace of adoption is running ahead of the rules meant to keep it in check, according to the announcement.

In practice, this means someone in the C-suite has to own the risk. Agent sprawl—where ad-hoc bots and assistants multiply across teams—makes it harder to track what these systems can do, what data they touch, and who is accountable when one misfires.

For ERP shops, the lesson is familiar: the technology isn't waiting for the policy. The governance models that worked for past rollouts may not survive contact with autonomous agents.
