Customer Engagements Shape Autonomous Enterprise Tools

PUBLISHED August 24, 2026 · NEWS / Industry Trend

The push toward autonomous enterprise is often described in grand product terms, but in practice it moves forward through customer projects. The recent announcement puts it simply: every engagement is a chance to learn, refine, and build something that can benefit an entire industry.

That logic flips the usual vendor playbook. Instead of standardizing everyone into the same mold, the focus is on pulling patterns from real implementations and carrying them to the next customer with similar needs.

For ERP teams, this suggests that roadmaps will keep getting shaped by what clients actually do with the software, not just by what vendors demo at trade shows. It also makes each implementation feel less like a one-off and more like a building block.
