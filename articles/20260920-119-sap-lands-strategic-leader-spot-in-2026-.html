SAP lands Strategic Leader spot in 2026 Fosway Cloud HR grid

PUBLISHED September 20, 2026 · NEWS / Recognition

SAP has been named a Strategic Leader in the 2026 Fosway 9-Grid for Cloud HR, according to the announcement. The 9-Grid is Fosway's analyst model for the Cloud HR market, and the placement puts SAP in a named recognition tier.

SAP tied the result to its ongoing investment in the HCM portfolio. The company said it is working to deliver the HCM platform organizations need to succeed in the future, though it did not spell out particular product changes in the summary.

In practice, analyst grid placements often come up during early vendor research. They can help frame a shortlist, but they are not a substitute for detailed requirements work. The announcement itself is narrow: a designation, plus SAP's stated investment rationale.
