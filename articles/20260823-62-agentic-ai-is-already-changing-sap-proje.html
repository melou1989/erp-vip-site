Agentic AI Is Already Changing SAP Project Economics

PUBLISHED August 23, 2026 · NEWS / Market Trends

Agentic AI is already on the ground inside SAP programs, not looming on a roadmap. That means the cost side of transformation is starting to look different.

According to the announcement, the real shift is economic. When software agents take over parts of configuration, testing, or data work, the usual ratio of effort to outcomes changes. That has consequences for how projects are budgeted and staffed.

The company said this isn't a waiting room scenario. The practical question now is which teams are structuring work around agents, and which ones are still pricing projects as if nothing moved.
