Finance vs. AI Tokens: The New Budget Balancing Act

PUBLISHED September 09, 2026 · NEWS / AI Cost Control

AI token spend is turning into a budget problem that finance can't ignore. The challenge, as described in the announcement, is to bring forecasting, allocation, and optimization to a cost that still feels unpredictable.

The harder part is cultural. If finance clamps down too tightly, AI experimentation stalls; if it ignores usage, spend slips out of control. The associated message says the real target is value — deciding what a token is worth before committing to it.

For ERP shops, that points to new routines: tracking token usage by team, by workflow, or by expected return. No one is saying AI should get a blank check, but the process needs to be fast enough to keep up with the pipeline.
