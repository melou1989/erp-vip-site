SAP Takes a Leader Spot in IDC's AI Order Management MarketScape

PUBLISHED August 13, 2026 · NEWS / Industry Recognition

The recognition puts SAP's order management suite among the top vendors evaluated by IDC for AI-driven order orchestration and fulfillment across retail and B2C operations.

IDC's MarketScape tracks how well vendor offerings align with both current needs and future direction. Being named a Leader suggests SAP's platform came out well on that comparison, according to the announcement.

SAP said it remains committed to developing its order management tools, especially around AI-enabled workflows. The announcement itself did not include deeper specifics on the assessment criteria or product roadmap.
