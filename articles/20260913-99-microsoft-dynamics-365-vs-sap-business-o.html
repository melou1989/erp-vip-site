Microsoft Dynamics 365 vs SAP Business One: ERP Comparison

PUBLISHED September 13, 2026 · COMPARISON / Vendor Comparison

Microsoft Dynamics 365 and SAP Business One get tossed into the same RFP more often than they should. Dynamics 365 is a suite—Business Central for smaller companies, Finance & Operations for larger ones. SAP Business One is one product, aimed squarely at small and midsize businesses. So the fair fight is Business Central vs Business One, but buyers rarely phrase it that way.

SAP Business One handles core accounting, inventory, purchasing, and basic manufacturing well. It’s been around long enough that partners know its quirks. Dynamics 365 Business Central covers similar ground but leans on Microsoft’s ecosystem: Outlook, Excel, Teams, Power BI. If your staff lives in Microsoft 365, the learning curve is shallower. If they don’t, that advantage evaporates.

Deployment choices differ. Business One has a long on-premise history and cloud options through hosting partners. Dynamics 365 is cloud-first, though Business Central can run on-premise. Microsoft pushes Azure, which means you get automatic updates and less server babysitting. SAP’s cloud story for Business One is less unified—partners host it, and the experience varies.

Customization is where implementations live or die. Business One uses SDKs and add-ons; heavy changes can break during upgrades. Business Central uses extensions and Power Platform, which are generally safer across versions. But neither is plug-and-play. Budget for a partner who knows the product, not just the platform.

Pricing? Both vendors charge per user, per month, and both have partner implementation fees that often dwarf license costs. SAP Business One sometimes looks cheaper upfront, but customizations and add-ons close the gap. Dynamics 365 subscriptions are transparent, yet you may need multiple apps. Neither is a bargain if you skimp on planning.

My take: if you’re a 50-user distributor already on Microsoft 365, Business Central is the easier sell. If you’re a 150-user manufacturer with complex inventory, SAP Business One and its partner network still deserve a look. Beyond a few hundred users, neither is the right long-term home—you’re in S/4HANA or Dynamics 365 Finance territory.
