Why AI's Next Bottleneck Is Sustainability

PUBLISHED October 04, 2026 · NEWS / Industry Trends

New York Climate Week 2026 put a sharper point on a tension building around AI: the technology's growth depends on resources, and those resources are under cost and climate pressure. Energy costs and physical risk were part of the conversation, along with AI's appetite for resources.

For ERP teams, the issue is practical. Sustainability metrics, energy spend and risk data often feed into the same planning and reporting systems that finance and operations rely on. When AI demand rises, those numbers get harder to separate from the broader cost base.

The event's framing did not offer a tidy answer. It suggested that sustainability is becoming a core business constraint for AI, not a communications exercise. ERP buyers should expect more pressure to track resource use and climate exposure alongside traditional financial measures.
