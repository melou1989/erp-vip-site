Scaling Enterprise AI Beyond the Individual User

PUBLISHED September 04, 2026 · NEWS / AI Strategy

The AI conversation in ERP has leaned toward individual convenience for a while: ask an assistant to draft a query, summarize an invoice, or guide a user through a screen. Those wins are real, but they tend to stay inside one person’s workflow.

The bigger challenge is institutional. AI should bridge the gap between what an individual user gets out of a tool and what the company as a whole gains from it. That means outputs need to reach shared processes, shape decisions, and eventually change operating procedures — not just remain one employee’s shortcut.

None of that happens by accident. It will require embedding AI results into formal workflows and governance. Whether the tools are ready for that shift is still an open question.
