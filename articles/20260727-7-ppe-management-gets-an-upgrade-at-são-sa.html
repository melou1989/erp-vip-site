PPE Management Gets an Upgrade at São Salvador with SAP Build

PUBLISHED July 27, 2026 · NEWS / Product Update

São Salvador Alimentos, a Brazilian food producer, has turned to SAP Build to bring its personal protective equipment management under control. According to the announcement, the company now handles PPE processes directly on the SAP low-code platform — a move that should reduce administrative overhead and help ensure gear is tracked properly from warehouse to production floor.

For a company in food processing, PPE compliance is not optional. The old system apparently required more manual effort. With SAP Build, the company said it can adapt workflows as regulations or equipment needs change, without heavy IT involvement. Honestly, that kind of flexibility matters when you're managing gloves, masks, and aprons across multiple shifts and lines.

SAP Build, which lets users create extensions and applications without deep coding expertise, seems like a practical fit for this kind of operational pain point. The project likely serves as a template for other manufacturers wrestling with similar PPE tracking challenges.
