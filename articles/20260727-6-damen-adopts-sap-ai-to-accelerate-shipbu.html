Damen Adopts SAP AI to Accelerate Shipbuilding

PUBLISHED July 27, 2026 · NEWS / AI Adoption

Damen Shipyards, the Dutch family-owned maritime group, is turning to SAP Business AI to modernize its shipbuilding operations. The company said the move is meant to cut design cycles and production delays — two perennial headaches in a industry where custom vessels can take years to complete.

According to the announcement, the AI tools will be integrated into Damen's existing SAP environment, targeting areas like engineering validation, supply chain planning, and production scheduling. Shipbuilders have traditionally relied on manual expertise and static blueprints; Damen is betting that machine learning can surface bottlenecks faster than humans can.

Honestly, this is a smart but cautious step. Shipbuilding isn't software — you can't patch a hull after launch. Damen isn't claiming the AI will revolutionize everything overnight, but the focus on measurable speed and accuracy suggests they're treating it as a practical tool, not a magic wand.
